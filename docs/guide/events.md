# Life events

Besides birth and death, a life is full of other moments worth recording: a baptism, school and
university, jobs, moving to another town or country, military service, a burial. Family Insight
calls them **life events**.

## Add an event

1. Select the person and click **Edit**.
2. Scroll to **Life events** and click **Add event**.
3. Choose the kind of event. They are grouped into religious events; work, school and service;
   moves and residence; and others:

<Screenshot name="events-menu" alt="The Add event menu with event types grouped as Religious, Work, school and service, Moves and residence and Other" />

The new event opens straight away, with the cursor in the date field.

## Fill in the details

<Screenshot name="events-editor" alt="Life events in the editor: Baptism and Military service; the military service is open with type, start date, end date (3) and place; buttons to edit (1) and move up (2)" />

- **Type** – change the kind of event at any time. For **Other event** type your own name (for
  example _Pilgrimage_).
- **Date (or start)** and **End date (optional)** – a single date, or a period such as military
  service from 1914 to 1918. Dates work like everywhere else: `1914`, `05.1914`, `~1914`
  ([more about dates](./editing#dates)).
- **Place** – suggestions come from places already used in the tree.
- **Description** – a short detail: the occupation, the school, the regiment, the ship.
- **Notes** – anything else.

Each field is saved when you leave it. Click an event's title or the pencil (1) to open or close it.

## Order and delete

- The arrows (2) move an event up or down. The order you choose is kept; in the person's timeline
  events are sorted by date anyway.
- The bin removes an event. A message with **Undo** appears – and like every change, it can also be
  undone with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>.

## Where events appear

- **Details and timeline** – the person's **Life** list shows events with their icon, date or period
  and place, between birth and death. Burial and cremation come after death.

  <Screenshot name="events-details" alt="The life timeline of Jan Kowalski: born, baptism, military service 1944–1945, education 1946–1951 and occupation 1951–1990" />

- **Search** – event places and descriptions are searchable: search for _Hamburg_ to find everyone
  who emigrated through Hamburg.
- **Statistics** – the most common life events and event places, and how many people have no events
  yet ([Statistics](./statistics)).

## Warnings

Like other dates, events get gentle warnings when something looks wrong – they never block you:

- _An event of … is dated before their birth._
- _An event of … is dated after their death._ (Burial and cremation are fine.)
- _This event ends before it starts._

::: info CSV and older versions
Life events are saved in the `.finsight` file. CSV exports contain one row per person and don't
include events. Since version 1.1, trees are saved in a newer file format that has room for events:
Family Insight 1.0 can't open them and asks you to update.
:::
