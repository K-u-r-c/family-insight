# Introduction

**Family Insight** is a free program for building your family tree, exploring it and keeping it
up to date. It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux, in eight languages (English, Polish, German,
French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Russian) and works completely offline.

## What you can do

- **Build the tree in a few clicks.** Add a person, then their father, mother, partner, children
  or siblings straight from their card. See [Adding people](./people).
- **Record real-life details.** Approximate dates such as “about 1890”, birth (maiden) surnames,
  places, notes, photos, adoptive and step-parents, marriages with dates and how they ended. See
  [Editing a person](./editing) and [Relationships](./relationships).
- **Explore comfortably.** Zoom, pan and a minimap; views of one person's ancestors or
  descendants; search that ignores Polish letters. See [Exploring the tree](./navigation).
- **Work with spreadsheets.** Import people from Excel, LibreOffice or Google Sheets and export
  them back as CSV. See [Import from CSV](./csv-import).
- **Print and share.** Save the tree as a picture or a PDF, or print it. See
  [Images, PDF and printing](./export-print).

## Where your data lives

Your tree is a single file with the extension **`.finsight`** that you save wherever you like –
for example in your Documents folder. There is no account and no cloud: Family Insight never
sends your data anywhere. Back the file up like any other document (a USB stick, an external
drive or your own cloud folder), and you can open it on any computer with Family Insight.

To protect you from losing work, the program also [saves automatically](./files#autosave),
keeps [backup copies](./files#backups) and can [restore unsaved work](./files#crash-recovery) after
a crash.

## Language and appearance

The whole program is available in **English**, **Polish**, **German**, **French**, **Spanish**,
**Italian**, **Portuguese** and **Russian**, with a **light** and a **dark** theme (or one that follows your system). You can switch both on the welcome screen or later in
the [settings](./settings).

## Where to start

1. [Install Family Insight](./install) on your computer.
2. Follow [Your first family tree](./first-tree) – it takes about ten minutes.
3. Or open the [sample family](./sample-family) and look around first.
