# Navigating the tree

## With the mouse or trackpad

- **Move** the tree by dragging an empty part of it.
- **Zoom** with the mouse wheel (or two fingers on a trackpad); zooming keeps the point under the
  pointer in place. Pinching on a trackpad zooms faster.
- **Click** a card to select the person, **double-click** it to edit, **right-click** it for more
  actions. Click an empty part of the tree to clear the selection.

When you select someone who is outside the visible area – for example in the list of people – the
tree moves to show them.

## Zoom controls and overview map

The buttons in the bottom-right corner:

<Screenshot name="canvas-controls" alt="The zoom controls: 1 zoom out, 2 current zoom (click for 100 %), 3 zoom in, 4 fit to screen, 5 centre on the selected person, 6 show or hide the overview map" />

1. **Zoom out**
2. **Current zoom** – click it to go back to 100 %.
3. **Zoom in**
4. **Fit to screen** – shows the whole tree.
5. **Centre on the selected person**
6. **Overview map** – shows or hides the small map in the bottom-left corner. The map shows the
   whole tree with a frame around the part you see; click or drag in it to jump elsewhere.

## Collapsing branches {#collapse}

Large trees are easier to read when you hide branches you are not working on. Point at a card that
has children and click the small **−** button under it to hide all of that person's descendants;
**+** shows them again.

<Screenshot name="collapse-branch" alt="The card of Tadeusz Wiśniewski with the small button under it (1) that hides his descendants" />

Collapsed branches are saved in the file together with the [view mode](./view-modes).

## With the keyboard {#keyboard}

Click the tree (or press <kbd>Tab</kbd> until the **Skip to the family tree** link appears and press
<kbd>Enter</kbd>), then:

| Key                         | Action                     |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| <kbd>↑</kbd>                | go to a parent             |
| <kbd>↓</kbd>                | go to a child              |
| <kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd> | go to a partner or sibling |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd>            | edit the selected person   |
| <kbd>F</kbd>                | fit the whole tree         |
| <kbd>+</kbd> / <kbd>−</kbd> | zoom in / out              |
| <kbd>0</kbd>                | zoom to 100 %              |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd>              | clear the selection        |

The first arrow key press selects the [root person](./view-modes#root-person) (or the first
person). All shortcuts are listed in [Keyboard shortcuts](./shortcuts).

::: info Screen readers
The tree works with screen readers: every card has a label with the name and
dates, and when you move with the arrow keys the program announces the person and their closest
relatives.
:::
