# Deleting and undoing

## Deleting a person

Select the person and click the **bin** button (🗑) at the top of the details panel, press
<kbd>Delete</kbd> (on a Mac <kbd>⌫</kbd>), or right-click their card and choose **Delete person**.
Family Insight asks first and tells you how many relationships will disappear with the person:

<Screenshot name="delete-confirm" alt="The question Delete Lena Kowalska? – Lena Kowalska will be removed together with 2 relationships – with the Cancel and Delete buttons" />

After deleting, a message with **Undo** appears for a few seconds.

## Undo and redo

Every change can be taken back – adding, editing and deleting people, relationships, renaming the
tree, setting the root person and imports:

| Action | Windows / Linux                                                               | macOS                                  |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Undo   | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>                                                  | <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>              |
| Redo   | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Y</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> | <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>⇧</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> |

You can also use the **Undo** and **Redo** buttons in the toolbar or the **Edit** menu. Family Insight
remembers the last 200 changes. Saving doesn't clear this history, but closing the tree does.

::: tip While typing in a field
Inside a text field, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> first undoes your typing in that field, like in any
other program. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to leave the field and undo whole changes.
:::
