# The main window

When a tree is open, the window looks like this:

<Screenshot name="workspace-tour" alt="The main window with numbered parts: 1 file menu, 2 Add person, 3 view modes, 4 search, 5 list of people, 6 details panel, 7 overview map, 8 zoom controls" />

1. **File menu** (☰) – new tree, open, save, save as, import and export CSV, export an image,
   [sources](./sources) and print. See [Saving and opening](./files).
2. **Add person** – adds a new person who isn't related to anyone yet. Next to it are **Undo** and
   **Redo**.
3. **View modes** – the whole family, or the ancestors, descendants or both (“hourglass”) of one
   person. See [View modes](./view-modes).
4. **Search** – finds people by name, place or year. See [Finding people](./search).
5. **List of people** – everyone in the tree, alphabetically. Click a name to select the person;
   the buttons at the top sort the list and hide it.
6. **Details panel** – the selected person: their dates, family and life story. Click **Edit** to
   change them. See [Editing a person](./editing).
7. **Overview map** – shows which part of the tree you are looking at; click or drag in it to move.
8. **Zoom controls** – zoom out, zoom in, back to 100 %, fit the whole tree and centre on the
   selected person. See [Navigating the tree](./navigation).

In the middle is the **family tree** itself. Click a card to select a person, double-click it to
edit, right-click it for more actions.

On the right of the toolbar are three more buttons: **Relationship calculator** (see
[Relationship calculator](./relationship-calculator)), **Statistics** (📊, see
[Statistics](./statistics)) and **Settings** (theme, language, card size and autosave, see
[Settings](./settings)).

The **status bar** at the bottom shows how many people the tree has, where the file is saved and
whether all changes are saved.

::: tip The menu bar
On Windows and Linux the window also has a classic menu bar (**File, Edit, View, Help**); on a Mac
it is at the top of the screen. It contains the same commands plus help, keyboard shortcuts and
information about the program.
:::

## Making room

- Drag the border between the list of people and the tree, or between the tree and the details
  panel, to make them wider or narrower. The program remembers the sizes.
- Hide the list of people with the button at its top (or **View → People List** in the menu bar)
  to give the tree more space.
- Close the details panel with **×**, or click an empty part of the tree.

## Guided tour {#guided-tour}

Choose **Help → Show guided tour** in the menu bar. A short tour points at the most important parts of the window one after another; you can use the program while it runs. Move with **Next** and **Back** (or the ← → keys) and press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to skip it. Finished or skipped tours are not offered again – **Settings → Reset tips and guided tours** brings them back.
