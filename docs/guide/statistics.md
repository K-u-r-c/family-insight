# Statistics

Click the **Statistics** button (📊) on the right of the toolbar, or choose **View → Statistics** in
the menu bar:

<Screenshot name="statistics" alt="The Statistics window of the sample family: 25 people, 16 living, 8 deceased, 5 generations, average lifespan 66 years, the most common given names, surnames and birth places and a list of people to complete" />

## The numbers

- **people** – everyone in the tree;
- **living** and **deceased** – by [life status](./editing#life-status) (people with an unknown
  status are in neither group);
- **generations** – the longest line from the oldest ancestor down to the youngest descendant;
- **average lifespan** – of deceased people with both a birth and a death date. Below it you see who
  lived longest; click **Show** to select that person.

## The most common names and places

Three lists show the five most common **given names** (the first given name counts), **surnames**
(the birth surname when there is one) and **birth places**. Click a bar to
[search](./search) for it – for example click **Łódź** to see everyone born there.

## Life events

Two more lists show the most common **life events** and **event places**; click a place to search
for it. Below them you see how many people have no life events yet ([Life events](./events)).

## To complete

The last section lists people who are **missing dates** (no birth or death date) or **missing
parents**. It's a handy to-do list for your research – click a name to go straight to that person.
When everyone has dates and parents, it says “Everyone has dates and parents. Nice work!”.
