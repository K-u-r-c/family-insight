# Finding people

Click the search box in the toolbar or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd> (on a Mac
<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd>) and start typing. The list of people shows only the matches, and in the tree
the matching cards are highlighted while everyone else fades:

<Screenshot name="search" alt="A search for lodz: the list shows 9 of 25 people and the matching cards are highlighted in the tree" />

## What you can search for

Search looks at **given names, surnames, birth surnames, places of birth and death, and the years
of birth and death**:

| You type      | Finds                                           |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| `kowal`       | Kowalski, Kowalska, Kowalczyk …                 |
| `lodz`        | everyone born or died in Łódź                   |
| `zolkiewski`  | Żółkiewski                                      |
| `1925`        | people born or died in 1925                     |
| `anna krakow` | people named Anna **and** connected with Kraków |

- **Letter case and Polish letters don't matter**: `lodz`, `Lodz` and `Łódź` give the same results.
- With **several words**, a person must match all of them.
- Parts of words work: `wiśn` finds Wiśniewski.

## Moving through the results

- Press <kbd>↓</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd> in the search box to jump to the first result in the list.
  Moving through the list with <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> selects each person in turn;
  <kbd>↑</kbd> on the first result takes you back to the search box.
- Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> in the search box (or click **×**) to clear the search.

## Sorting the list

The **sort** button at the top of the list of people orders it **by surname**, **by given name** or
**by date of birth**:

<Screenshot name="people-sort" alt="The sort menu of the list of people with By surname, By given name and By date of birth" />

Names are sorted the Polish way (Ł after L, Ś after S) when the program is in Polish.
