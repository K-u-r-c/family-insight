# Details and timeline

Click a person to see their details in the panel on the right:

<Screenshot name="timeline" alt="The details of Anna Kowalska née Wiśniewska with her dates, family and the Life timeline" />

At the top are the name, the birth surname, the years of life and the **age**:

- for living people (or when the status is unknown) the current age, for example “71 years old”;
- for deceased people the age at death, for example “died aged 87”;
- “about …” when a date is partial or approximate.

Below are the person's **family** (see [Adding people](./people)) and their **life timeline**.

## The life timeline

**Life** lists everything that happened in the person's life, in order:

- **Born** – date and place of birth;
- **Married** (or partnered, engaged) – with the partner's name, date and place;
- **Child born** – each child, by date of birth;
- **life events** – baptism, education, occupation, military service … with their date or period
  ([Life events](./events));
- **Divorced**, **Separated from**, **Widowed** – when a partnership ended;
- **Died** – date and place of death, followed by burial or cremation.

Birth always comes first and death last; events without a date are shown in a natural order with
“Date unknown”. Click a name in the timeline to go to that person.

Events come from the person's own dates and from their relationships – so the timeline fills up as
you add [partnerships](./relationships#partners) and children. **Notes** are shown
under the timeline.
