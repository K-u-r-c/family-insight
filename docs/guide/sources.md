# Sources

Good family history says **where each fact comes from**: a parish register, a civil record, a census,
a family letter, an interview with grandma. Family Insight keeps a list of your **sources** and lets
you **cite** them for every fact.

## Cite a source for a fact

1. Select the person and click **Edit**.
2. Next to **Name** (sources about the person in general), **Birth**, **Life** (the death) and every
   life event there is a small source button (1). It shows how many sources the fact has.

<Screenshot name="cite-buttons" alt="The editor of Jan Kowalski with a small source button next to Birth (1) showing one source" />

3. In the window that opens, choose an **existing source** – or type the title of a **new source** and
   click **Create and cite**.
4. Add the details of the citation (click the pencil to open it):
   - **Page or entry** – where in the source it is, for example _p. 12_ or _entry 345_;
   - **Reliability** – _primary_ (written at the time, like a birth record), _secondary_ (written
     later, like a family chronicle), _questionable_ or _unreliable_;
   - **Date of the record**, a **transcription** of the text as written, and **notes**.

<Screenshot name="citations-dialog" alt="The Sources window for Jan Kowalski's birth with one citation: the parish register, entry 345, primary, with the transcription of the record" />

Relationships can be cited too: open the **⋯** menu next to a parent or partner and choose
**Sources**. In **Edit partnership** you can cite the start (for example the marriage record) and the
end separately.

## Manage your sources

Open **☰ → Sources…** (or **Edit → Sources…** in the menu bar):

<Screenshot name="sources-manager" alt="The Sources manager: a searchable list of sources on the left and the selected parish register with title, repository, call number and the facts that cite it on the right" />

- **Search** and **New source** on the left; click a source to edit it on the right.
- **Title**, **Author** and **Publication** describe the source; **Call number** is its signature in
  the archive.
- **Repository** – where the source is kept: an archive, a library, a parish office, a website. Pick
  one from the list or add a new one under **New repository**.
- **Cited by** lists every fact that cites the source – click one to go to that person.
- **Delete source** – if the source is cited, Family Insight asks first and removes its citations
  too. Like every change, it can be undone.

## Where you see sources

- **Details** – facts with sources get a small number after them (1). Click it to see the citations.
  At the bottom, **Sources** lists every source used for the person.

  <Screenshot name="sources-details" alt="The details of Jan Kowalski: a marker 1 after Born (1) and a Sources list at the bottom with three sources" />

- **Search** finds people by the titles of their sources.
- **Statistics** show the share of facts that have at least one source – a good measure of how well
  documented your tree is ([Statistics](./statistics)).

::: info
Sources are saved in the `.finsight` file. CSV exports don't include them.
:::
