# View modes

The **view mode** buttons in the toolbar decide who is shown in the tree:

<Screenshot name="view-controls" alt="The four view mode buttons: 1 Family, 2 Ancestors, 3 Descendants, 4 Hourglass" />

1. **Family** – everyone in the tree.
2. **Ancestors** – one person, their parents, grandparents and so on.
3. **Descendants** – one person, their children, grandchildren and so on (with their partners).
4. **Hourglass** – ancestors and descendants of one person together.

(On a wide window the buttons also show their names.) The same choices are in the **View** menu of
the menu bar.

## Whose ancestors? {#root-person}

The ancestor, descendant and hourglass views start from the **root person** – marked with a small
house (🏠) on their card. If the tree has no root person, they start from the **selected** person.

To make someone the root person, right-click their card and choose **Set as root person**. The
same menu offers **Unset root person**. In the [sample family](./sample-family) the root person is
Jan Kowalski.

## How many generations? {#generations}

In the ancestor, descendant and hourglass views a list next to the buttons sets how many generations
to show – from **1 generation** to **All generations**. Fewer generations make large trees easier to
read and print.

## Saved with the file

The view mode, the number of generations and [collapsed branches](./navigation#collapse)
are saved in the `.finsight` file and restored the next time you open it. Changing the view is not
an edit, so it doesn't appear in [undo](./undo).
