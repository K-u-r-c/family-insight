# Photos and documents

Each person can have a **gallery** of photos and scanned documents: wedding pictures, certificates,
letters, PDFs from an archive. They are stored **inside the `.finsight` file**, so the tree stays one
file you can copy anywhere – and a picture used for several people is stored only once.

## Add photos and documents

1. Select the person. The details show **Photos and documents** below the notes.
2. Click **Add…** (1) and pick one or more files – or drag them from your file manager onto the
   section.

<Screenshot name="media-gallery" alt="The details of Jan Kowalski with the Photos and documents section: Add… (1) and four items – a wedding photo, the family house, a letter and a PDF birth certificate – each with a ⋯ menu (2)" />

Supported are **JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF** and **PDF** files up to **25 MB** each. Family Insight checks
what a file really is, not just its name. Images get a small preview; PDFs show a document icon.

Each item has a **⋯** menu (2):

| Menu item              | What it does                                                                          |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Edit details…**      | Title, date, place and description – see below.                                       |
| **Use as portrait…**   | Opens the [photo cropper](./editing#photo); the cropped picture becomes the portrait. |
| **Move earlier/later** | Changes the order of the gallery.                                                     |
| **Remove from …**      | Takes the item out of this person's gallery. Like every change, it can be undone.     |

## Look at a photo or document

Click an item to open the viewer:

<Screenshot name="media-viewer" alt="The viewer showing the wedding photo of Jan and Anna with its title, 1 of 4, June 1950, Łódź, zoom controls at the bottom and the description below" />

- **Zoom** with the mouse wheel, the **+**/**−** buttons or a double-click; **drag** to move around;
  the button next to them fits the picture to the window again.
- **←** and **→** (or the arrows at the sides) go to the previous and next item of the gallery.
- **PDF documents** open in the built-in PDF viewer, where you can scroll, zoom and search them.
- The buttons at the top **edit the details**, **open the file in another program** (for example
  your PDF reader), and **save a copy** of the file anywhere.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the viewer.

## Describe it and show it on several people

**Edit details…** (also in the viewer) describes the item and decides **whose gallery** shows it:

<Screenshot name="media-details" alt="The Photo or document window for the wedding photo: title, date June 1950, place Łódź, description, and Shown on: Jan Kowalski and Anna Kowalska with Add person… (1)" />

- **Title**, **date** (partial and approximate dates work as everywhere –
  [Dates](./editing#dates)), **place** and **description** – who is in the picture, what the document
  says. The viewer and the gallery show them.
- **Shown on** lists the people whose gallery has the item. A family photo can be shown on everyone
  in it: click **Add person…** (1) and pick them. **×** hides it from one person again.
- **Save a copy…** exports the original file. **Delete from the tree** removes the item from every
  gallery, source and citation (you are asked first if it is used anywhere).

## Scans of sources {#scans-of-sources}

A scan of a register page or a certificate belongs to the [source](./sources) it shows. In
**☰ → Sources…**, select a source and use **Attach…** under **Scans and photos** (1); in the
[citation window](./sources#cite) each citation can have its own scans too.
Click a scan to open it in the viewer.

<Screenshot name="source-scans" alt="The Sources manager with the parish register selected and a PDF attached under Scans and photos (1)" />

::: info Saving and file size
Pictures are kept in their original quality, so a tree with many scans grows accordingly – a few
hundred megabytes are no problem and saving stays quick. CSV exports don't include photos and
documents.
:::
