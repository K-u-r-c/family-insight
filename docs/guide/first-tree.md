# Your first family tree

In about ten minutes you will build a small tree of three generations – you, your parents, your
partner and your child – add a photo and save it. Use your own family or follow the example.

## 1. Create the tree

On the welcome screen click **New family tree**. Give your tree a name – for example your family
name – and click **Create**. You can rename it later by clicking the name in the toolbar.

<Screenshot name="new-tree" alt="The Name your family tree dialog with the name field (1) and the Create button (2)" />

## 2. Add yourself

The tree starts with one person and their editor is already open on the right. Fill in:

<Screenshot name="first-person" alt="A new tree with Anna Kowalska; the editor on the right with given names (1), surname (2) and date of birth (3) filled in" />

1. **Given names** – for example `Anna`.
2. **Surname** – for example `Kowalska`. If your surname changed (for example after marriage),
   click **Add birth surname** and enter the one you were born with.
3. **Date of birth** – a year (`1990`), a month (`05.1990`) or a full date (`12.05.1990`). Not
   sure? Type `~1990` for “about 1990”. See [Dates](./editing#dates) for all the options.

Choose your **sex** too – it decides the colour of your card and helps with the next steps.
Each field is saved as soon as you move to the next one; click **Done** when you are finished.

## 3. Add your parents

The details panel now shows your **family**: parents, partners, children and siblings, each with
**+** buttons.

<Screenshot name="family-section" alt="The family section of a person with numbered buttons: 1 Father, 2 Mother, 3 Partner, 4 Child and 5 the link button for choosing someone already in the tree" />

1. Click **+ Father**. A new person appears in the tree, connected to you, and their editor opens.
   The **surname is already filled in** from yours (in Polish in the masculine form, so the father
   of Anna Kowalska becomes Kowalski) – just type the **given names**.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-father" alt="The editor of the new father: the given names field (1) is empty and ready for typing, the surname (2) is already Kowalski" />

2. Click **Done**, then go back to yourself: click your card in the tree, or your name in the
   father's **Children** list.
3. Click **+ Mother** and type her names. Because you already have a father, your parents are
   connected as a couple automatically.

::: tip Someone is already in the tree?
Use the link button (🔗) next to a group instead of **+** to choose a person who is already in the
tree – for example when you added your grandmother earlier. See
[Linking someone already in the tree](./people#linking-existing).
:::

## 4. Add your partner and child

Select yourself again and:

1. Click **+ Partner** and type their names.
2. Click **+ Child**. The child is connected to you **and** to your partner. The surname is taken
   from the father. If you have more than one partner, Family Insight asks who the other parent
   is.

Your tree now has three generations:

<Screenshot name="tutorial-result" alt="A tree with Jan and Maria Kowalski at the top, Anna Kowalska and her partner Tomasz Nowak below them and their daughter Zofia Nowak at the bottom" />

## 5. Add a photo (optional)

Select a person, click **Edit**, then **Add photo…** in the editor and choose a picture (JPG, PNG or
WebP). Drag the picture to position the face, use the slider to zoom and click **Use photo**. You
can also drop an image file onto the round photo placeholder.

<Screenshot name="photo-crop" alt="The Crop the photo dialog with a round frame over a portrait, a zoom slider and the Use photo button" />

## 6. Save your tree

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd> (on a Mac <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>), or open the **☰** menu and
choose **Save**. The first time, choose a folder and a file name – the file gets the extension
`.finsight`. From then on, Family Insight [saves automatically](./files#autosave) after every
change, and the status bar at the bottom says **All changes saved**.

## Made a mistake?

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>) or the **Undo** button in the toolbar to
take back the last change – as many steps as you need. See [Deleting and undoing](./undo).
