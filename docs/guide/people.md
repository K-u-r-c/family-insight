# Adding people

## Add a person

Click **Add person** in the toolbar (or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd>, on a Mac
<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>⇧</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd>). A new card appears in the tree and its editor opens so you
can type the name. The new person isn't connected to anyone yet – usually it's quicker to add
relatives as described below.

## Add relatives in one click

Select a person (click their card or their name in the list). The details panel shows their
**family** in four groups – **Parents**, **Partners**, **Children** and **Siblings** – each with
**+** buttons:

<Screenshot name="family-section" alt="The family section of Katarzyna Kowalska with numbered buttons: 1 Father, 2 Mother, 3 Partner, 4 Child, 5 the link button" />

1. **+ Father** and
2. **+ Mother** – shown while that parent is missing. When both parents are known, the group
   offers **+ Parent** instead, which adds a **step-parent** (you can change the type later, see
   [Relationships](./relationships)).
3. **+ Partner** – a husband, wife or partner. A person can have several partners.
4. **+ Child** – a son or daughter.
5. The **link button** (🔗) next to each group connects someone who is
   [already in the tree](#linking-existing).

The **+ Sibling** button appears under **Siblings**; it needs at least one parent, because siblings
are people with the same parents.

Every click creates the new person, connects them and opens their editor, so you only type the
name. Family Insight fills in what it can:

| You add   | What happens automatically                                                                                                                                                                |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| a father  | He gets the family surname (the birth surname if there is one; Polish feminine forms become masculine: Kowalska → Kowalski). If the person already has a mother, the two become a couple. |
| a mother  | If the person already has a father, the two become a couple.                                                                                                                              |
| a partner | The sex is preset to the opposite of the person's (you can change it).                                                                                                                    |
| a child   | The child is connected to the person **and** their partner. With several partners you are asked who the other parent is. The surname comes from the father.                               |
| a sibling | The sibling gets the same parents and the family surname.                                                                                                                                 |

<Screenshot name="which-parent" alt="The question Who is the other parent? with the partners Magdalena Nowicka and Katarzyna Kowalska and the option Unknown / nobody" />

## Linking someone already in the tree {#linking-existing}

Sometimes the relative is already in your tree – for example you entered your grandmother on your
mother's side and now want to connect her to your aunt too. Click the **link button** (🔗) next to the
right group, then search for the person and click their name:

<Screenshot name="link-existing" alt="The Choose a parent dialog with a search field and a list of people" />

The list doesn't offer people who are already in that group. If the connection is impossible – for
example someone would become their own ancestor – Family Insight explains why and nothing changes.

## From the tree

Right-click a card for the same actions: edit, add a relative, set
the person as the [root person](./view-modes#root-person) or delete them.
