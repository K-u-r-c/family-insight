# Map

The map shows **where your family lived**: every birth, death, marriage and life event at a place
that has a [location](./places). Switch between the tree and the map with the buttons next to the
view modes in the toolbar (or **View → Map**, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>M</kbd>).

## Read the map

- Each **marker** is a place; the number tells how many people have a fact there. Places close to
  each other are grouped into a circle with a number at small zoom – zoom in to separate them.
- The **colour** shows the generation, counted from the tree's root person: warm colours for
  earlier generations, cool colours for later ones, grey for the root's own generation (3).
- **Click a marker** (2) – or select it with <kbd>Tab</kbd> and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> – to list
  everyone with a fact there. Click a name to select the person: the details open, and the
  **dashed line** shows the places of their life in order (birth, events by date, death).
- **Zoom** with the mouse wheel or the **+**/**−** buttons, drag to move around.

## Filter

- **Show** (1) – births, deaths, marriages and other life events, in any combination.
- **Period** – drag the two sliders to show only facts between two years. Facts without a date are
  hidden while a period is set.

## Places without a location

Below the filters, Family Insight lists the places that don't have a location yet. **Set location**
opens them in the [Places](./places) window, where you can look them up or enter the coordinates.

## Online maps {#online}

The map background – streets, rivers, borders – comes from
[OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org) on the internet. It is **off until you allow it**:

<Screenshot name="map-offline" alt="The notice that the map background comes from OpenStreetMap on the internet and is off, with the button Load maps online" />

- Click **Load maps online** and confirm. Only the map area you look at is downloaded – no names or
  other data from your tree. Map pieces are kept on your computer for a week.
- Without it, or without an internet connection, the markers, filters and lists still work on a
  plain background.
- You can switch it off again in the settings (**⚙ → Load maps online**).

## Export

**Export map as PNG…** saves the map as you see it – with the markers, the selected path and the
OpenStreetMap credit – as an image.
