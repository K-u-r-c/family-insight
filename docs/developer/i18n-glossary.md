## Kinship terms

The relationship calculator names relationships with per-language rules in
`src/core/kinship/terms/<code>.ts` (tests: the 33 fixture relationships in both sexes). The terms
follow these references; notable choices are listed with each language. Relationships the
language has no word for (more than 12 generations, or unusual step and in-law shapes) get a
sentence instead: the common ancestors and the generations up and down (`distant`), or "related
by marriage" (`byMarriage`); `distant.test.ts` lists them for every language.
